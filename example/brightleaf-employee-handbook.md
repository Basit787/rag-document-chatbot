# Brightleaf Robotics — Employee Handbook

Brightleaf Robotics is a fictional company used to test the RAG Document
Chatbot. Every fact below is made up.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Thursday. Outside core
hours, people choose their own schedule. Friday is a "focus day": no internal
meetings may be booked on Fridays.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams meet in the
Lisbon office every Tuesday, called "Anchor Tuesday". Fully remote contracts
need written approval from the Head of People, currently Marta Okonkwo.

## Time off

- Annual leave: 27 working days per year, plus local public holidays.
- Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year; they expire on 31 March.
- Sick leave: no fixed limit. A doctor's note is needed after 4 consecutive
  days.
- Parental leave: 20 weeks fully paid for every parent, usable within 18
  months of the birth or adoption.
- Volunteering: 2 paid days per year for registered charities.

Leave requests go through the "Sprout" HR portal at least 10 working days in
advance for anything longer than 3 days.

## Equipment

Each new hire chooses a laptop from the approved list (a 14-inch or 16-inch
model). The home-office budget is EUR 600 once, plus EUR 150 every two years
for replacements. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24
hours at `it-help@brightleaf.example`.

## Expenses

- Meals while travelling: up to EUR 45 per day.
- Hotels: up to EUR 160 per night, EUR 210 in London, Zurich and Oslo.
- Train is required for trips under 4 hours; flights need manager approval.
- Receipts must be uploaded within 30 days of the expense.

## Learning budget

Every employee gets EUR 1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences.
Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## Security rules

1. Use the company password manager for every work account.
2. Two-factor authentication is mandatory on email, code hosting and the HR
   portal.
3. Customer data must never be copied to personal devices.
4. Report suspected phishing with the "Report" button in the mail client.

## Probation and reviews

The probation period is 4 months. Performance reviews happen twice a year, in
April and October. Salary bands are reviewed every January.
