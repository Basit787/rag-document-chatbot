SUNSPROUT S2 SMART PLANTER - USER MANUAL
(Fictional product, made up for testing the RAG Document Chatbot.)

1. WHAT IS IN THE BOX
- SunSprout S2 base with 6 planting pods
- LED grow-light arm (adjustable, 3 height positions)
- 12 V power adapter
- 6 seed capsules: basil, mint, parsley, cherry tomato, chili, lettuce
- Nutrient bottle, 100 ml (lasts about 3 months)

2. SPECIFICATIONS
- Water tank: 4.2 litres
- Light: 24 W full-spectrum LED, 3 brightness levels
- Default light cycle: 16 hours on, 8 hours off
- Weight: 2.1 kg (empty)
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz only (5 GHz networks are not supported)
- Warranty: 2 years on the base, 1 year on the LED arm

3. FIRST SETUP
Step 1. Fill the tank with room-temperature water up to the MAX line.
Step 2. Add 10 ml of nutrient for a full tank.
Step 3. Insert seed capsules and cover each with a germination dome.
Step 4. Plug in the adapter. The status ring glows white while starting.
Step 5. Open the SunSprout app and hold the leaf button for 5 seconds to pair.
Remove the germination domes once sprouts touch them (usually day 7 to 10).

4. STATUS RING COLOURS
- White, steady: normal operation
- Blue, pulsing: pairing mode
- Yellow, steady: water level low (below 1 litre)
- Orange, blinking: add nutrients (every 14 days)
- Red, blinking: pump error - see section 6

5. CARE
- Top up water when the ring turns yellow; never fill above MAX.
- Add 10 ml of nutrient every 14 days.
- Clean the tank with warm water and a drop of vinegar every 2 months.
- Prune herbs from the top so they grow bushier; do not remove more than one
  third of a plant at once.
- Cherry tomato and chili need the light arm in the highest position after
  week 5.

6. TROUBLESHOOTING
Problem: red blinking ring.
  Cause: pump blocked or tank empty. Fill the tank, then hold the leaf button
  for 2 seconds to restart the pump. If it still blinks, unplug for 30 seconds.
Problem: the planter will not pair.
  Cause: usually a 5 GHz network. Connect your phone to a 2.4 GHz network and
  retry. A factory reset is done by holding the leaf and light buttons together
  for 10 seconds.
Problem: seeds have not sprouted after 21 days.
  Cause: old capsule or domes removed too early. Replacement capsules are free
  within the first 3 months.
Problem: leaves turn yellow.
  Cause: too much nutrient. Empty the tank, rinse, refill with fresh water and
  skip nutrients for one cycle.
